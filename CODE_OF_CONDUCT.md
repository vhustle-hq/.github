# Code of conduct

These rules apply to every Vhustle space on GitHub: issues, comments and any other place we host conversation.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Stay on topic: one problem or idea per issue.
- Keep other people's information private, and your own.

## Not allowed

- Harassment, insults, slurs or personal attacks.
- Sexual content, violent threats or encouraging self-harm.
- Sharing anyone's personal information, including health data, without consent.
- Spam, advertising, or repeated off-topic posts.
- Medical claims presented as advice to others. Vhustle is not medical advice, and neither are its issues.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or delete content that breaks these rules, and may block people from the organization. Serious or repeated breaks lead to a permanent block.

To report a problem with someone's behaviour, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue.
