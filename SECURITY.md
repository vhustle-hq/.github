# Security policy

Thank you for helping keep Vhustle and the people who use it safe.

## How to report

Report security problems privately through [GitHub's private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/vhustle-hq/feedback/security/advisories/new).

Never report a security problem in a public issue, discussion or pull request. Public reports are deleted.

## What to include

- What the problem is and what an attacker could do with it.
- Steps to reproduce it, and the app version, platform or web page affected.
- Any proof of concept. Use only your own account and data.

## What we ask

- Give us reasonable time to fix the problem before you share it with anyone.
- Don't access, change or delete other people's data, and stop as soon as you've confirmed the problem.
- Don't run tests that degrade the service for others, such as denial of service or spam.

## What we do

We confirm that we received your report, keep you updated while we work on it, and tell you when it's fixed.
